# One Line to Your Younger Self — contest entry

This repository holds my submission to the make.fun competition [One Line to Your Younger Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-one-line-to-your-younger-self-22e97f76).

## What gets submitted

> The embarrassing thing you replay at 2am? Nobody else remembers it - they were busy replaying their own.

## Why this sentence

Three candidate lines were drafted, then scored against the priorities spelled out in the competition RULES: a stranger should grasp the line on a single read, after which surprise, brevity, originality, and a smooth read-aloud all count. This one was picked because its comfort comes from a reversal — the memory that feels uniquely shameful turns out to be something everyone is too preoccupied with their own version of to even notice. The runner-up drafts lost out for reading as a list of tips and as a motivational slogan, respectively.

## Running it

Executing `main.py` with Python 3 produces `entry.txt` containing exactly the sentence above. The script leans only on the standard library, touches no network, and yields identical output on every run; the destination folder comes from the `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable, falling back to `/output`.
